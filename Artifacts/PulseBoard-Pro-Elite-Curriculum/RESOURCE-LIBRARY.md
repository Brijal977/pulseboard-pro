# The Resource Library — Everything You Need to Learn It All

A complete, curated learning library for the whole curriculum — books, free courses, paid courses, video series, interactive sites, docs, channels, newsletters, and practice platforms — mapped phase by phase, then cross-cutting resources at the end.

> **Read this first — how to use it.** This is a *menu, not a checklist.* You do **not** need everything here; you need *enough* to build the phase and defend it. For each section I mark the highest-ROI pick with ⭐ — if you only touch one thing, touch that. Free-first is the rule: buy the few paid items that are genuinely worth it (marked below), and only when you'll actually use them. Depth over completeness — one book read properly beats ten skimmed.

**Legend:** 🆓 Free · 💵 Paid (one-time) · 🔁 Subscription · 📗 Book · 🎥 Video/course · 🖥️ Interactive/hands-on · 📄 Docs/reference · ⭐ Highest ROI in its section

**A note on prices & recency:** exact prices and course availability change, and newer resources may exist beyond my knowledge — verify before buying. The canonical books and free sites listed are stable and battle-tested.

## 📚 The Foundational Bookshelf (career-long — not tied to one phase)

These are the books that shape how you *think*, worth returning to for years. Don't binge them; read one slowly alongside the phases.

- ⭐ 📗 **The Pragmatic Programmer** — Hunt & Thomas 💵 — the closest thing to a mentor in book form. Start in Phase 0, a chapter a week.
- ⭐ 📗 **A Philosophy of Software Design** — John Ousterhout 💵 — thin, profound, on complexity and deep modules. Pairs with Phases 1, 5, 9.
- ⭐ 📗 **Designing Data-Intensive Applications (DDIA)** — Martin Kleppmann 💵 — the single most important backend book. Central to Phases 6 & 9; you'll reread it for years.
- 📗 **The Staff Engineer's Path** — Tanya Reilly 💵 — what seniority past senior actually is. Phase 12.
- 📗 **Software Engineering at Google** — Winters, Manshreck, Wright 🆓 (free online at abseil.io) — how a top org handles review, testing, scale, culture.
- 📗 **Refactoring** — Martin Fowler 💵 — the vocabulary and discipline of improving code safely.
- 📗 **Code Complete** — Steve McConnell 💵 — encyclopedic craftsmanship classic.
- 📗 **The Software Engineer's Guidebook** — Gergely Orosz 💵 — modern, practical career navigation across levels.
- 📗 **Clean Code** — Robert Martin 💵 — widely read; *read critically* — some advice is dated/debated. Take the mindset (naming, small functions), question the dogma.

### Final mentor note on all of this
The abundance above is a trap if you let it be — collecting resources *feels* like progress and isn't. The rule stands: **build first, read second, and never read without building it the same session.** Pick the ⭐ item for the phase you're in, use it to get unstuck and go deeper, then get back in the editor. The library is here for when you hit a wall — not to be consumed front to back. You could read every book on this page and still be junior; you become elite by *building* PulseBoard and *defending* every decision. Everything here is in service of that.
